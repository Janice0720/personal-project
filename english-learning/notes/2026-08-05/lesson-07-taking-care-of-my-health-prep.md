# 課前準備 — Lesson 07：Taking Care of My Health

> 課堂筆記：（課後補連結）

---

## 本週想達成的目標

1. 能用英文自然說出我最近在做哪些健康保養習慣（運動、飲食、保健品）。
2. 練習「原因解釋」句型：用 *not because… but because…* 說明動機，讓表達更立體。
3. 確認保健品相關字彙的發音與用法（supplements、probiotics、lutein）。

---

## 分享草稿

<details>
<summary>原始初版（保留備查）</summary>

Recently, I have been more serious about taking care of my body.

I try to work out once a week, and I'm also trying to lose weight in a healthy way. I don't want to go on an extreme diet, so I just try to eat more protein and vegetables, and eat fewer snacks.

I also take some supplements every day. In the morning, I usually take fish oil, lutein, vitamin D3, and vitamin C. At night, I sometimes take collagen and probiotics.

For me, taking supplements is not about treating a disease. It is more like a self-care habit. I want to keep my body and skin in better condition, and I think having a routine makes me feel healthier and more disciplined.

</details>

### 潤稿版（帶去課堂分享）

Recently, I've been paying more attention to my health.

I try to work out once a week, and I'm managing my weight by watching what I eat — eating more protein and vegetables, and cutting back on snacks. Nothing too extreme; I'm just trying to make small adjustments.

I also take supplements every day. In the morning, I take fish oil, lutein, vitamin D3, and vitamin C. At night, I sometimes take collagen and probiotics.

It's not because I'm sick — it's more of a self-care routine. I want to keep my body and skin in better condition, and I find that having a routine makes me feel more disciplined and energized.
