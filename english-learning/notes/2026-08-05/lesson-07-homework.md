## 我的初稿

The most impotant thing that truelly make my life better is airpod. The best function is the noise gate. Since I'm a sesitive person, I need more me time to focus on everything.

---

## 文法修正

| 原句 | 問題 | 建議 |
|------|------|------|
| impotant | 拼字錯誤 | **important** |
| truelly | 拼字錯誤 | **truly** |
| that truelly make | 主詞 "thing" 為單數，動詞須加 s | that truly **makes** |
| airpod | 專有名詞需大寫且為複數 | **AirPods** |
| the noise gate | 術語錯誤（noise gate 是錄音工程用語）| **noise cancellation**（主動降噪）或 **noise isolation** |
| The best function is… | function 指「功能運作」，這裡說的是產品特色 | The best **feature** is… |
| sesitive | 拼字錯誤 | **sensitive** |
| more me time to focus on everything | "me time" 指放鬆的個人時間，語意偏掉；"focus on everything" 邏輯矛盾（focus 是聚焦一件事） | **space to stay focused** / **time to concentrate without distractions** |

---

## 修改後完整版

The most important product that truly makes my life better is my AirPods. The best feature is noise cancellation. Since I'm a sensitive person, I need to block out distractions to stay focused — whether I'm on the MRT or working at my desk.

---

## 自我複習重點

- **noise cancellation**（降噪）≠ noise gate（錄音工程用語）；另可說 **noise isolation**（隔音）
- 單數主詞 + that 子句 → 動詞記得加 s：*the thing that **makes***
- "**me time**" = 放鬆的個人時間（We all need some me time on weekends.）≠ 專注工作的時間
- "**feature**" 指產品特色、功能亮點；"function" 指運作功能，兩者常搞混
- "**block out distractions**" = 隔絕干擾，比 "ignore noise" 更自然口語
- "**stay focused**" = 保持專注，可替換 "concentrate" / "keep my concentration"
