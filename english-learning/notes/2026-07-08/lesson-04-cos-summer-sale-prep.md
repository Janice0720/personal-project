## 分享草稿 — 生活小故事（COS 夏季特賣）

I also want to share a small story from this week.

I went to the COS summer sale. It started in mid-June and ended yesterday. There was a really cute shoulder bag that I wanted, and many people were talking about it on Threads.

Since I work nearby, I went to the store almost every day to see if it was back in stock. But I still couldn't get it, so I felt a little regretful.

I'm not a shopping addict, but the discount was really good, so I think it was worth checking.
