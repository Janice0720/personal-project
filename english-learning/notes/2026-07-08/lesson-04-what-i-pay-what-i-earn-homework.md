## 文法修正

> 以下針對口語版（最終繳交稿）進行分析。

| 原句 | 問題 | 建議 |
|------|------|------|
| I **also** had this experience. | `also` 放在助動詞前語法正確，但口語中 `too` 置句尾或 `I, too,` 置主詞後更自然 | I had this experience **too**. / I, too, went through this. |
| **But** later, I realized that… | 句首用 `But` 口語可接受；正式寫作建議改 `However` | **However**, I later realized that… / **Over time**, I realized… |
| avoid making the same mistakes **again** | `same` 已含「重蹈覆轍」之意，`again` 略顯重複（但口語很常見，不算錯） | avoid making the same mistakes（可省略 again） |

## 修改後完整版

If I had the chance to teach someone a topic, I would like to teach Asian children how to face mistakes.

I think many Asian children are raised to be careful, conservative, and afraid of making mistakes. In many families, parents push their children to improve all the time. Of course, they usually do this because they love their children and want them to have a better future. However, this kind of pressure can also make children afraid of failure.

I once heard Jensen Huang, the CEO of NVIDIA, say something like, “If you were raised by Asian parents, you may need therapy for life.” I think this sentence is funny, but it also reflects something real. Many Asian children grow up feeling that they must always be perfect.

That was also how I felt when I was younger. After I graduated and started working, I was extremely afraid of making mistakes at work. I was worried that one mistake would prove that I was not good enough. Because of this, I sometimes became too careful and conservative.

However, I slowly learned that mistakes are not always bad. In real life and at work, what matters is not avoiding every mistake. What matters is learning how to solve problems after making mistakes, understanding why the mistakes happened, and finding ways to avoid the same mistakes next time.

So, if I could teach someone, I would teach them that making mistakes does not mean they are failures. Mistakes are part of learning, growth, and problem-solving. We should not be careless, but we also should not be controlled by fear. The most important thing is to learn from mistakes and become better each time.


### 口語版

If I had the chance to teach someone a topic, I would like to teach Asian children how to face mistakes.

I think many Asian children are raised to be very careful and afraid of making mistakes. Their parents often push them to improve, and of course, most parents do this because they love their children. However, this pressure can make children feel that they always have to be perfect.

I also had this experience. After I graduated and started working, I was very afraid of making mistakes at work. I worried that if I made a mistake, people would think I was not good enough. Because of that, I sometimes became too careful and conservative.

But later, I realized that mistakes are part of learning. We should not be afraid of mistakes. Instead, we should learn how to solve problems, understand why the mistakes happened, and avoid making the same mistakes again.

So, I would like to teach children that making mistakes does not mean they are failures. It means they are learning and growing.

## 自我複習重點

- **也** 的三種說法：`I also + V`、`I + V + too`、`I, too, + V`——口語首選 `too` 置句尾
- **But vs. However**：句首 `But` 口語沒問題；正式段落用 `However,` 或 `Over time,`
- **避免重複**：`same mistakes again` 中 `again` 可省；同理 `past history`、`future plans` 也有此問題
- **好用句型整理**：
  - *…are raised to be very careful and afraid of making mistakes.*
  - *This pressure can make children feel that they always have to be perfect.*
  - *I worried that if I made a mistake, people would think I was not good enough.*
  - *Making mistakes does not mean they are failures. It means they are learning and growing.*
