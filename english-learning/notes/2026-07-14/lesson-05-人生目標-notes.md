## 老師的關鍵糾正

| 學生原句 | 老師建議 | 說明 |
|---------|---------|------|
| "a little company" | "a small-scale company" | little 指體積小，規模小要說 small-scale（或 small company） |
| "sometimes in the future" | 不說 sometimes，改 "someday" 或 "one day in the future" | sometimes 有「偶爾」的意思，放在「未來」前語意模糊，老師說「you scare me」 |
| "I can found the one I want" | "I can find out / figure out" | found 是 find 的過去式，在此應用 find out 或 figure out |
| "最近" | "recently" | 老師直接示範：*just say "recently"* |
| 課前草稿內容 | 老師無逐句糾正 | 老師理解內容並自然延伸討論，未提出文法修正 |

---

## 文法重點

- **小規模公司**：a small-scale company ／ a small company，不說 a little company（little 多指體積）
- **未來某天**：someday ／ one day in the future，不說 sometimes in the future（sometimes = 偶爾）
- **表達熱情**：*I'm passionate about data analysis.* ／ *What keeps you passionate about this?*
- **目標拆解句型**：*What do I need to buy a house? → How much is the down payment? → How much do I need to save each month?*
