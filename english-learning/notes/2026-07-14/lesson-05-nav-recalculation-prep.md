## 分享草稿 — 職場事件（修訂版）

This week felt like a math class because of a recent fund incident in Taiwan.

Cathay Securities Investment Trust had to recalculate the NAV of eight funds. The main reason was related to internal control and related-party transaction issues. One of their directors was also an independent director of another listed company, but some funds or accounts managed by Cathay had traded that company's stock.

Because of this, the fund NAVs had to be recalculated, and affected investors needed to be compensated.

Since my company is one of the sales channels, we had to help with the operational work. We needed to split the accounts and calculate how much compensation each customer should receive. We had to check transaction records, affected fund holdings, and the difference for each investor.

It was very detailed and a little stressful, but also interesting. It showed me how important accuracy, compliance, and internal controls are in the financial industry.
