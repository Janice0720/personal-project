## 題目一：What do you need to do to buy a home?

### 我的初稿

Since I graduated from university, I've always wanted to buy a house. Not just any house — I want to live next to my family. It might be a very big dream, but I'm taking it step by step. My first goal is to buy a small studio apartment of around 10 ping to use as an investment property. Right now, I already set aside around NT$20,000 each month for investing. It's still far from my goal, but I believe that small amounts add up over time, and someday I'll be able to get there.

---

### 文法修正

| 原句 | 問題 | 建議 |
|------|------|------|
| Right now, I **already** set aside around NT$20,000 each month for investing. | `right now` 和 `already` 意思重複，語氣累贅 | 二選一：(1) Right now, I set aside around NT$20,000 each month for investing. (2) I've already been setting aside around NT$20,000 each month for investing. |

---

### 修改後完整版

Since I graduated from university, I've always wanted to buy a house. Not just any house — I want to live next to my family. It might be a very big dream, but I'm taking it step by step. My first goal is to buy a small studio apartment of around 10 ping to use as an investment property. Right now, I set aside around NT$20,000 each month for investing. It's still far from my goal, but I believe that small amounts add up over time, and someday I'll be able to get there.

## 題目二：What are the qualities necessary for a long and happy partnership?

### 我的初稿

For me, the most important quality in a partner is that we need to have the same values. If two people have completely different values, it's very hard to build a happy relationship. Besides that, I think a good partner should be self-disciplined and hold themselves to high standards. People who are disciplined also tend to take care their health and appearance — like work out regularly and keeping a good image. I also think a partner should be responsible for the family and be willing to put the family first. And last but not least, having a good temper and being tolerant is very important. A long-term relationship will definitely have disagreements, so being able to stay calm and accept each other's differences is the key to stay together.

---

### 文法修正

| 原句 | 問題 | 建議 |
|------|------|------|
| tend to **take care** their health | `take care` 後面需要加介系詞 `of` | tend to **take care of** their health |
| like **work out** regularly | `like` 後面接動名詞，不是原形動詞 | like **working out** regularly |
| the key to **stay** together | 這裡的 `to` 是介系詞（不是不定詞），後面要接動名詞 | the key to **staying** together |

---

### 修改後完整版

For me, the most important quality in a partner is that we need to have the same values. If two people have completely different values, it's very hard to build a happy relationship. Besides that, I think a good partner should be self-disciplined and hold themselves to high standards. People who are disciplined also tend to take care of their health and appearance — like working out regularly and keeping a good image. I also think a partner should be responsible for the family and be willing to put the family first. And last but not least, having a good temper and being tolerant is very important. A long-term relationship will definitely have disagreements, so being able to stay calm and accept each other's differences is the key to staying together.

---

## 自我複習重點

- `take care of` 是固定搭配，`of` 不可省略
- `like / enjoy / avoid` 這類動詞後面接動名詞（V-ing），不接原形動詞
- `the key to` 中的 `to` 是介系詞，後面用 V-ing（vs. `the key is to + V` 中的 `to` 才是不定詞）

### 文法提醒

- `right now` + `already` 一起用會重複，二選一即可
- 若要強調「已經持續在做」，用現在完成進行式：**I've already been setting aside...**
- `small amounts add up` 是固定搭配，也可說 **every little bit counts**（每一點都算數）
- `step by step` 比 `one step at a time` 更口語，兩者都正確
- `take care of` 的 `of` 不可省略，這是最常見的錯誤之一
- 區分 `the key to V-ing`（to 是介系詞）vs `the key is to V`（to 是不定詞）
