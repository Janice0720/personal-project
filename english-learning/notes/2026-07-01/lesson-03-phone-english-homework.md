# 作業 — Lesson 03：職場電話英文 — 接聽國外機構來電與專業界線

**題目**：How do you quickly understand what a customer needs? And how do you set boundaries?
**繳交日期**：下堂課討論

---

## 我的初稿

When I need to understand what a customer needs, I usually start by asking a few questions to clarify what kind of help they are looking for. Then, I will identify whether the issue involves personal information.

If it involves personal information, I need to verify the customer's identity before I can provide any details or take further action. If it does not involve personal information, I can often answer their question directly or guide them to our FAQ page and existing resources.

Setting boundaries is also very important in my job. For example, we are not allowed to recommend specific funds to customers. When a customer asks, "Do you have any fund recommendations?" I usually explain that we cannot provide investment recommendations. Instead, I suggest that they use the tools on our website to search for and compare funds based on their own investment goals and risk preferences.

For me, the key is to be helpful while still following compliance rules and protecting the customer's personal information.

---

## 文法修正

| 原句 | 問題 | 建議 |
|------|------|------|
| Then, I **will identify** whether the issue involves personal information. | 描述習慣性動作時用 `will` 顯得像預測未來，語感偏生硬 | Then, I **determine** / **I then check** whether the issue involves personal information. |
| I suggest that they **use** the tools | `suggest that + 主詞 + 動詞原形` 本身正確（假設語氣），口語中也常見 `suggest + Ving`；兩者皆可，但需保持一致 | 可保留原句；或改為 I suggest **using** the tools on our website... |
| …based on their own investment goals and **risk preferences** | `risk preferences` 可以用，但金融場景更常見 `risk tolerance`（風險承受度）或 `risk appetite` | …based on their own investment goals and **risk tolerance**. |

## 修改後完整版

When I need to understand what a customer needs, I usually start by asking a few questions to clarify what kind of help they are looking for. I then determine whether the issue involves personal information.

If it involves personal information, I need to verify the customer's identity before I can provide any details or take further action. If it does not involve personal information, I can often answer their question directly or guide them to our FAQ page and existing resources.

Setting boundaries is also very important in my job. For example, we are not allowed to recommend specific funds to customers. When a customer asks, "Do you have any fund recommendations?" I usually explain that we cannot provide investment recommendations. Instead, I suggest using the tools on our website to search for and compare funds based on their own investment goals and risk tolerance.

For me, the key is to be helpful while still following compliance rules and protecting the customer's personal information.

---

## 自我複習重點

- **描述習慣性流程**用現在簡單式，不用 `will`：I determine / I check / I identify
- **金融術語**：`risk tolerance`（風險承受度）比 `risk preferences` 更道地
- **suggest 的用法**：`suggest that + S + V原形`（假設語氣）或 `suggest + Ving`，兩者皆正確但同段落需一致
- **好用句型**：*I then determine whether…* / *I usually explain that we cannot…* / *The key is to be helpful while still following…*
