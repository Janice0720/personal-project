# 課前準備 — Lesson 08：Korea Trip & Medical Aesthetics

> 課堂筆記：（課後補連結）

---

## 本週想達成的目標

1. 能用英文自然說出這次去韓國的動機——不是容貌焦慮，而是好奇心與保養體驗。
2. 練習介紹自己在台灣的保養習慣，銜接到「為什麼對韓國醫美有興趣」。
3. 學會描述選診所的過程：搜尋評價、比較、安排行程。

---

## 分享草稿

I'm going to Korea at the end of this month. I'm not the kind of person who has anxiety about my appearance, but I'm interested to try medical aesthetics in Korea because I heard they are very good and cheaper than Taiwan.

### 潤稿版（帶去課堂分享）

I'm heading to Korea at the end of the month — and one thing I'm really looking forward to is trying out a Korean aesthetic clinic.

It's not because I have any insecurities about my appearance. I'm genuinely just curious. I already do regular skin treatments back in Taiwan — I get pore-cleansing facials and laser treatments — so it's not my first time with this kind of thing. I just want to see what the Korean experience is like, since their aesthetic industry is so well-developed, and the prices are said to be much more affordable.

Right now, I'm in the middle of planning my itinerary and researching clinics. I've been reading through reviews to find one with a solid reputation.

## 課前預查單字

| 中文 | 英文說法 | 備註 |
|------|----------|------|
| 韓國醫美 | Korean aesthetic clinic / medical aesthetics in Korea | aesthetic = 美學的、醫美的 |
| 醫美診所 | aesthetic clinic / cosmetic clinic | 非手術類的叫 non-surgical procedure |
| 容貌焦慮 | appearance anxiety / body image insecurity | insecurity about my appearance（更口語） |
| 不是因為容貌焦慮 | It's not because I have insecurities about my appearance | 或：I'm not doing it out of insecurity |
| 純粹好奇 | I'm genuinely just curious / out of pure curiosity | genuinely 讓語氣更真誠 |
| 定期保養 | do regular skin treatments / go for regular facials | regular = 定期、固定的 |
| 粉刺清潔 | pore-cleansing facial / blackhead extraction | extraction 指實際清出粉刺的動作 |
| 臉部雷射 | laser facial / laser skin treatment | 泛指雷射類療程 |
| 業界發達 | well-developed industry / advanced aesthetic industry | 也可說：Korea is known for its beauty industry |
| 價格實惠 | more affordable / better value | affordable 比 cheap 更有禮貌 |
| 安排行程 | plan my itinerary / plan my trip | itinerary（行程表）是進階詞彙，很好用 |
| 搜尋評價 | read reviews / research clinics online | going through reviews（逐一看評價）更自然 |
| 評價不錯 | good reviews / solid reputation | solid = 扎實的、可靠的，比 good 更有說服力 |
| 體驗看看 | try it out / see what it's like | try out 強調嘗試新體驗 |
| 做臉 | get a facial | facial 當名詞，I got a facial. |
