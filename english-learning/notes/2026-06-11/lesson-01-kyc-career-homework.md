## 修改後完整版

If I had to design a containment system for wealthy customers with many unreasonable requests, I would create a VIP service system through a LINE Official Account.

I noticed that many customers use smartphones to review their investment portfolios and invest frequently. In Taiwan, many people already use LINE every day, so it could be a convenient channel for customer service.

The first thing I would do is develop a simple LINE plugin or mini service. Customers could log in to their accounts, check their investment portfolios, review transaction records, and complete simple investment-related tasks.

More importantly, the system would classify customer requests into different categories, such as account issues, investment questions, transaction problems, or urgent requests. Simple questions could be handled by self-service tools or a chatbot. More complicated requests could be transferred to a customer service representative or relationship manager.

This system would help customers receive faster service, while also preventing unreasonable requests from taking too much time from the company. It would create clear service boundaries and help the company manage customer requests more efficiently.

## 自我複習重點

- **containment system**：管控機制，用於限制和處理特定問題
- **investment portfolio**：投資組合（比 inventory 更精確的說法）
- **LINE Official Account**：LINE 官方帳號
- **relationship manager**：客戶關係經理
- **classify... into categories**：將⋯⋯分類
- **self-service tools**：自助服務工具
- 動詞形式：*The first thing I would do is **develop**（原形）*，不加 -ing
- 句子要有完整主詞：*This would allow customers to...*（不能直接 Provide customer...）
