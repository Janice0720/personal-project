### 潤稿版（帶去課堂分享）

I've been getting ready for a trip to Korea this week. The problem is, there's a typhoon coming, so my flight might be affected.

Yesterday I was scrambling to get travel insurance — and then last night, the insurance company announced that they're no longer accepting applications for August 27th and 28th. So if my flight can't take off because of the typhoon, I need to figure out whether I should buy another ticket in advance, or just postpone my departure by one day.

## 課前預查單字

### 颱風與天氣

| 中文 | 英文說法 | 備註 |
|------|----------|------|
| 颱風 | typhoon | 亞洲用 typhoon；大西洋用 hurricane；印度洋用 cyclone |
| 颱風要來了 | there's a typhoon coming / a typhoon is approaching | approaching = 接近中 |
| 受到颱風影響 | be affected by the typhoon | My flight might be affected. |
| 惡劣天氣 | bad weather / severe weather | severe = 嚴重的 |
| 因天氣取消／延誤 | weather-related cancellation / weather delay | 保險與航空公司常用這組字 |
| 不可抗力 | force majeure | 合約／保險用語：天災等無法控制的情況 |

### 飛機起飛、延誤、改班

| 中文 | 英文說法 | 備註 |
|------|----------|------|
| 起飛 | take off | 動詞片語：The plane couldn't take off. |
| 降落 | land / landing | We landed two hours late. |
| 出發 | departure / depart | delay my departure = 延後出發 |
| 班機 | flight | my flight might be delayed / cancelled |
| 延誤 | delay / delayed / run late | The flight was delayed by three hours. |
| 取消 | cancel / cancellation / be cancelled | My flight was cancelled due to the typhoon. |
| 停飛、無法起飛 | grounded / can't take off | Planes are grounded because of the typhoon. |
| 受困、走不了 | stranded | I might get stranded at the airport. |
| 改期 | reschedule | reschedule my flight to the next day |
| 延後一天出發 | postpone my departure by one day | postpone = 延後；by one day = 一天 |
| 提前出發 | leave a day earlier / bring the trip forward | 相對 postpone |
| 再買一張機票 | buy another ticket / book an extra flight | in advance = 提前 |
| 候補、改搭別班 | take an alternative flight / get on a later flight | alternative = 替代的 |
| 緊急處理 | scramble to… / handle something last-minute | I was scrambling to get insurance. |
| 辦理登機 | check in / boarding | boarding = 登機；check-in 報到 |

### 保險與理賠

| 中文 | 英文說法 | 備註 |
|------|----------|------|
| 旅遊保險 | travel insurance | 出國前買的那一種 |
| 投保、買保險 | get / take out travel insurance | take out a policy = 投保一份保單 |
| 保單 | (insurance) policy | 保險合約本身 |
| 保費 | premium | 你付給保險公司的錢 |
| 承保範圍 | coverage / what's covered | Am I covered if the flight is cancelled? |
| 有保到／沒保到 | covered / not covered | Typhoon delays may not be covered. |
| 不受理申請 | no longer accepting applications | 也可說：they stopped taking applications |
| 8/27–8/28 不受理 | they're not accepting applications for Aug 27–28 | for + 日期 = 針對那兩天 |
| 排除條款、不保項目 | exclusion / excluded dates | 某些日期被排除在承保外 |
| 理賠 | a claim / file a claim / make a claim | file a claim = 提出理賠申請 |
| 理賠金 | payout | They approved the claim and issued a payout. |
| 拒賠 | deny a claim / reject a claim | The claim was denied. |
| 行程取消險 | trip cancellation coverage | 因故去不成，申請退費用這個 |
| 行程中斷 | trip interruption | 已經出發但中途被迫中斷 |
| 截止時間 | cutoff / deadline | They announced the cutoff last night. |

## 可能的延伸話題

- 你原本幾號出發、待幾天？診所或行程改得動嗎？
- 如果再買一張機票，兩張都飛不成怎麼辦？
- 老師遇過颱風或天氣害班機取消嗎？當時怎麼處理？
- 買旅遊保險時，你通常會看哪些承保項目？
