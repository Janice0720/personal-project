## 文法修正

| # | 原句 | 問題 | 建議 |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | unasseptable | 拼寫錯誤 | **unacceptable** |
| 2 | behaiver | 拼寫錯誤 | **behavior** |
| 3 | "on a parner" | 介系詞錯誤（partner 用 *in*）＋拼寫錯誤 | "**in a partner**" |
| 4 | "emotional reation" | 拼寫錯誤；語意不夠精準（reaction = 單次反應，這裡指「容易情緒化」的特質） | "**emotional reactivity**"（情緒反應過度的傾向） |
| 5 | "so that if I have a emotional boyfriend" | ① *so that* 引導「目的」，這裡應用 *so* 引導「結果」 ② *a emotional* → 母音前應用 *an* | "so if I had **an** emotional boyfriend" |
| 6 | vocano | 拼寫錯誤 | **volcano** |
| 7 | "a stable sentiment" | *sentiment* 指「情感/看法」，語意偏；穩定情緒狀態應說 *emotional stability* | "**emotional stability**" |
| 8 | "in every conmunicate" | 拼寫錯誤；*communicate* 是動詞，此處需名詞 | "in every **interaction**" 或 "in all **communication**" |
| 9 | "must have ambitious" | *ambitious* 是形容詞，不能接在 *have* 後；應用名詞 *ambition* | "must have **ambition**" |
| 10 | "on his own life foreground" | *foreground* 是「前景（畫面）」，不是「前途」 | "for his own **future**" |
| 11 | "no matter it is about" | 連接詞用法不自然 | "**whether** it's about" |
| 12 | "The person who has ambitious" | 同 #9，詞性錯誤 | "**An ambitious person**" |
| 13 | "cause many things keep working" | 句構不完整 | "**keeps everything moving forward**" |
| 14 | "how many failures he get" | 主謂不一致（he → gets）；用 *face* 更自然 | "how many failures he **faces**" |
| 15 | "he's keep moving on" | 動詞形式錯誤（be + 原形不合法） | "he **keeps** moving on" |
| 16 | "if he keep whining" | 主謂不一致 | "if he **keeps** whining" |
| 17 | "don't do nothing" | 雙重否定（英文只能否定一次） | "**doesn't do anything**" |
| 18 | "ti change" | 拼寫錯誤 | "**to** change" |

## 修改後完整版

The most unacceptable behavior in a partner is emotional reactivity. I'm a sensitive person, so if I had an overly emotional boyfriend, it might feel like a volcano situation — two sensitive people constantly clashing. Emotional stability is always important in every interaction.

Second, he must have ambition for his own future, whether it's about his career, health, or appearance. An ambitious person keeps everything moving forward. It doesn't matter how many failures he faces, as long as he keeps moving on.

Last but not least, whining doesn't help anything. Sometimes it may be acceptable, but if he keeps whining without doing anything to change the situation, that is the worst thing.

## 自我複習重點

1. **ambitious (adj.) vs. ambition (n.)**：*have* 後面接名詞 → "have **ambition**"，不能說 "have ambitious"
2. **so that vs. so**：*so that* 表目的（為了...），*so* 表結果（因此...）；表達「所以如果…」用 *so*
3. **a vs. an**：母音（a / e / i / o / u）開頭的詞前用 *an* → "**an** emotional boyfriend"
4. **雙重否定**：英文不能同時否定 → "don't do **nothing**" 要改成 "doesn't do **anything**"
5. **情緒相關精準詞**：*emotional stability*（情緒穩定）、*emotional reactivity*（情緒反應過度）比 *stable sentiment* 更地道
