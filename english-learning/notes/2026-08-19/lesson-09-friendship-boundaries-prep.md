# 課前準備 — Lesson 09：Friendship, Punctuality & One-sided Friends

> 課堂筆記：（課後補連結）

---

## 本週想達成的目標

1. 能用英文清楚說出三件事：聚會遲到、有事才聯絡，以及被當成情緒垃圾桶、還被推去當中間人。
2. 練習表達界線與感受，而不是只抱怨——說出「我在意什麼」與「為什麼覺得壓力不該由我扛」。
3. 跟老師討論：這種朋友該怎麼相處？要不要直接拒絕幫忙傳話，還是慢慢拉開距離？

### 潤稿版（帶去課堂分享）

It's been a spectacular week... and I mean that sarcastically.

I had two gatherings this weekend — one with my high school friends and one with my college classmates. What I couldn't believe is that people showed up late to **both** of them. The worst was the college one: someone was a whole hour late. We get along well, and I do think of them as good friends, but punctuality actually matters to me. I'm not talking about being ten or fifteen minutes late — that happens. An hour, though, feels a bit much. It makes me feel like my time isn't being respected.

There's another college friend I wanted to talk about. She barely keeps in touch with us most of the time. It feels like she only reaches out when she needs a favor. For example, she was going abroad and needed someone to look after her plants. When none of us could help, she just disappeared from the group chat.

This past week was the hardest part. I've known for a while that she's been dating my colleague, but she never told me — and neither did our other classmates. I also happen to know that my colleague isn't a good match for her. She only came clean last week, after she couldn't find him. That really hurt. She doesn't bother to stay in touch in everyday life, but when she's heartbroken, that's when she comes to me for company.

I even spent two hours on the phone with her, just to calm her down. And since that day, she's been messaging me every day, kind of wallowing — saying she feels sick, she can't eat, she needs a hug. I feel like I've been absorbing a lot of negative energy.

After we met over the weekend, I mentioned a few reasons why this colleague isn't a good match. Then she started saying she feels disgusted just thinking about him. Yesterday she even asked me to tell my colleague to return her access card. That felt like she was asking me to break up with him on her behalf. I didn't sign up for this — I feel like I've been put in the middle and taking on a lot of pressure for no reason.

## 課前預查單字

| 中文 | 英文說法 | 備註 |
|------|----------|------|
| 非常精彩的一週（反話） | It's been a spectacular week. / What a week. | 反話開場；可加 *and I mean that sarcastically* 或 *not in a good way* |
| 出現、到場 | show up / showed up | 片語：出現、現身。People showed up. = 人們到了；showed up late = 遲到到場 |
| 守時 | punctual / on time | punctuality matters to me（我很在意守時） |
| 遲到 | show up late / run late / be late | run late 較口語：Sorry I'm running late. |
| 讓人等一小時 | keep someone waiting for an hour | 比 be late for an hour 更強調「對方在等」 |
| 遲到一小時太誇張 | an hour late is a bit much / over the top | a bit much = 有點過分（語氣比 too much 柔） |
| 遲到 10–15 分鐘還好 | I don't mind 10–15 minutes | I can live with being a little late. |
| 不尊重我的時間 | it feels like my time isn't being respected | 把「生氣」講成「感受」，比較成熟 |
| 相處不錯 | we get along well | get along 指相處融洽，不一定很親 |
| 幾乎不聯絡 | barely keeps in touch / hardly ever reaches out | keep in touch = 維持聯絡 |
| 有事才聯絡 | only reaches out when she needs a favor | favor = 幫忙、拜託 |
| 單向的友誼 | a one-sided friendship | 也可說：it feels transactional（像在交易） |
| 幫忙顧植物 | look after / water her plants / plant-sit | plant-sit 仿 babysit，口語很好用 |
| 從群組消失 | disappeared from the group chat / went quiet | go quiet 較溫和：she just went quiet |
| 隱瞞交往 | she never told me they were dating / she kept it from me | keep something from someone = 隱瞞 |
| 坦白說出 | come clean / tell me the truth | come clean 口語：終於說實話 |
| 不是適合的對象 | not a good match / not a suitable partner | a good match 比 suitable 更自然 |
| 覺得很受傷 | that really hurt / I felt left out | left out = 被排除在外 |
| 失戀才來找我陪 | only comes to me when she's heartbroken | 也可說：she only shows up when she needs emotional support |
| 來找人陪 | for company | 片語：為了有人作伴。come to me for company = 來找我陪；keep someone company = 陪某人 |
| 把我當情緒垃圾桶 | treat me like an emotional dumping ground | 較重；課堂可用 softer 版：only comes to me when she needs to vent |
| 講了兩小時電話安撫她 | spent two hours on the phone just to calm her down | comfort her / talk her down 也可以 |
| 無病呻吟 | wallowing / making a fuss / fishing for sympathy | wallowing = 沉浸在難過裡；fishing for sympathy = 博取同情 |
| 吃不下、需要抱抱 | she can't eat, she needs a hug | 課堂可加：she's being quite dramatic |
| 吸收負面情緒 | absorb a lot of negative energy / take on her emotions | 也可說：it's been draining |
| 覺得噁心 | she feels disgusted just thinking about him | disgusted 比 sick 更準；grossed out 更口語 |
| 磁扣／門禁卡 | access card / key fob | 公寓磁扣常用 access card 或 key fob |
| 請他把磁扣還給同學 | ask him to return her access card | return something to someone |
| 要我去當傳話的人 | put me in the middle / make me the messenger | 經典句：Don't shoot the messenger. 反過來說：I don't want to be the messenger. |
| 好像要我去幫她分手 | break up with him on her behalf | on her behalf = 代替她 |
| 無端承受壓力 | taking on a lot of pressure for no reason | 也可說：I didn't sign up for this.（我沒答應要扛這個） |
| 設界線 | set a boundary / set boundaries | I need to set some boundaries. |
| 拉開距離 | keep some distance / take a step back | I'm thinking of taking a step back. |
| 拒絕幫她傳話 | I'm not going to pass that message for her | 直接、清楚，適合當邊界句 |

## 可能的延伸話題

- 對你來說，朋友遲到多久可以接受？會不會當面講？
- 「有事才聯絡」的朋友，還算不算朋友？
- 好朋友交往對象是你認識的人，應不應該主動告訴你？
- 對方失戀來找你陪時，你會怎麼回？直接陪伴，還是先講清楚感受？
- 每天聽她無病呻吟，什麼時候該說「我沒辦法再聽了」？
- 朋友要你去跟第三者傳「還東西／分手」這種話，你會拒絕嗎？怎麼說才不傷和氣？
- 老師自己遇過類似的朋友嗎？他會怎麼處理？
