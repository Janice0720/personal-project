## 文法修正

| # | 原句 | 問題 | 建議 |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | recently , i really think about | ① 最近持續在想的事，用現在完成進行式較自然 ② *think about* 後面不能直接接完整子句 *i must…* | **Recently, I've been thinking that…** |
| 2 | i must spent time | *must* 後面接原形動詞；*spent* 是過去式 | I must **spend** time |
| 3 | no matter just om my career but | ① 想講的是「不只是…而是…」，不是 *no matter*（無論）② *om* 是 *on* 的拼寫錯誤 | **not just on** my career, **but also** |
| 4 | my health my mind my skin | 列舉三項時要用逗號，最後一項加 *and* | my health, my mind, **and** my skin |
| 5 | 直得我投資的事 | ① 中文應為「值得」② 英文用 *worth + V-ing* | anything that can make me better is **worth investing in** |
| 6 | 永遠對不會的事情保持好奇 | 「不會的事」＝還沒會的事；*curious* 搭配 *about* | **stay curious about** things I don't know yet |
| 7 | 並永遠持續學習 | 「永遠持續」用 *keep + V-ing* 即可，跟課堂 *keep thinking* 同一句型 | **and keep learning** |
| 8 | 永遠謙虛 | *humble* 是形容詞，前面要有動詞 | **Always stay humble.** |
| 9 | 看別人的優點並且學習他的好 | ① 英文 *look at* / *see* ② 「他的好」＝優點 *strengths / good qualities* ③ 單數 *his* 改成複數 *their* 較通 | Look at other people's **strengths** and **learn from their good qualities**. |

---

## 修改後完整版

What truly matters to me throughout my entire life are these three things.

First, recently I've been thinking that I must spend time on myself — not just on my career, but also on my health, my mind, and my skin. Anything that can make me better is worth investing in.

Second, I always want to stay curious about things I don't know yet, and keep learning.

Third, I always want to stay humble — look at other people's strengths and learn from their good qualities.

---

## 自我複習重點

1. **must + 原形**：must 後面不加過去式 → *must spend*，不是 *must spent*
2. **not just… but also…**：表達「不只是 A，還有 B」；*no matter* 是「無論」，意思不一樣
3. **worth + V-ing**：值得做某事 → *worth investing in* / *worth spending time on*
4. **stay + 形容詞**：保持某種狀態 → *stay curious* / *stay humble*（跟課堂 *keep thinking* 一樣，都是「一直維持」）
5. **keep + V-ing**：持續做 → *keep learning*；課堂金句就是 *Keep thinking is always important.*
